CURRICULUM

Structured 6-Phase Reading Schedule

Our canonical chronological reading plan distributes Scripture evenly (~12–14 chapters daily, ~45–60 minutes) to take you from Genesis to Revelation in 92 days.

Phase 1 Days 1–15

The Law & Pentateuch

Genesis 1 to Deuteronomy 34 (187 chapters)

Creation, the Fall, God's covenant with Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, the Exodus from Egypt, Sinai Law, the Tabernacle, and Israel's wilderness wanderings.

Phase 2 Days 16–35

Historical Books

Joshua 1 to Esther 10 (249 chapters)

The Promised Land conquest, period of the Judges, King David, King Solomon, the Divided Kingdom, Babylonian exile, and the return to rebuild Jerusalem under Ezra, Nehemiah, and Esther.

Phase 3 Days 36–49

Poetry & Wisdom Literature

Job 1 to Song of Solomon 8 (243 chapters)

Suffering and God's sovereignty in Job, 150 Psalms of heartfelt prayer and worship, practical wisdom in Proverbs, meaning in Ecclesiastes, and marital love in Song of Solomon.

Phase 4 Days 50–64

Major & Minor Prophets

Isaiah 1 to Malachi 4 (250 chapters)

Messianic prophecies, divine righteousness, warnings of exile, lamentations, visions of the new covenant, and promises of the coming Redeemer.

Phase 5 Days 65–76

The Gospels & Acts

Matthew 1 to Acts 28 (117 chapters)

The life, miracles, teachings, crucifixion, and resurrection of Jesus Christ across the four Gospels, followed by the birth and global expansion of the early Church in Acts.

Phase 6 Days 77–92

Epistles & Revelation

Romans 1 to Revelation 22 (143 chapters)

Apostolic teachings on grace, faith, discipleship, and holy living from Romans to Jude, concluding with the glorious triumph of King Jesus in Revelation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 92-Day Bible Reading Plan and application features.

What is the Project Bible in 92 Days challenge? +

Project Bible in 92 Days is an interactive Scripture accountability platform and reading challenge organized by The Youth Gathering 2026 (@tg.youth_). It guides readers through all 66 books and 1,189 chapters of the Holy Bible in 92 days (August 10 to November 9, 2026).

How many chapters do I need to read each day to finish the Bible in 92 days? +

Readers read approximately 12 to 14 chapters per day, requiring about 45 to 60 minutes of daily reading or audio narration.

Which Bible translations are supported in the app? +

The platform features 7 major English translations with instant toggling: NIV (New International Version), ESV (English Standard Version), NLT (New Living Translation), KJV (King James Version), NKJV (New King James Version), WEB (World English Bible), and NASB (New American Standard Bible).

Can I listen to today's reading portion with audio narration? +

Yes! The app features a built-in Audio Bible narrator with 4 voice profiles (US and UK accents), adjustable speed modulation (0.75x to 2.0x), background playback, Screen Wake Lock API keep-alive, and lockscreen media controls via the MediaSession API.

How does squad accountability and the streak freeze system work? +

Readers log daily completions to maintain reading streaks and level up through 10 Disciple XP tiers. Every 5 days of reading earns 1 Streak Freeze token (capped at 3 maximum), which automatically protects streaks if a reader misses a day.

Can visitors preview the reading plan without an account? +

Yes! Visitors can preview the entire 92-day reading schedule, daily portions, FAQs, and test-drive the application using instant guest access.

Is Project Bible in 92 Days free to use? +

Yes, Project Bible in 92 Days is 100% free and open-source, created for Christian youth discipleship and community Scripture reading.
