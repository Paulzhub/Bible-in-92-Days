How to Install on iPhone & iPad (Safari)

Apple iOS requires saving through Safari's native Share menu. No App Store login or download wait needed!

1
Open Link in Safari
Navigate to paulzhub.github.io/Bible-in-92-Days/ in the Safari browser. (If viewing inside Instagram or WhatsApp, tap the Safari compass icon in the corner first).
2
Tap the Share Button
At the bottom of your Safari screen, tap the Share icon (a blue square with an arrow pointing straight up ⎙).
Located at bottom-center toolbar
3
Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down the sharing sheet options and tap Add to Home Screen (marked with a square ➕ icon).
Scroll past Copy & Bookmark
4
Tap "Add" in Top-Right Corner
Confirm by tapping Add at the top right. The golden Bible 92 app icon will now appear directly on your home screen!

How to Install on Android (Chrome & Samsung Internet)

Android provides seamless one-tap installation directly from Google Chrome or Samsung Internet.

A
Method 1: Tap the In-App 'Install App' Button
On our website header right below Plan Overview, tap the golden 📲 Install App button.
Fastest 1-Tap Method
B
Tap "Install" on the Prompt
A native Android dialog will appear asking "Install app? Project Bible in 92 Days". Tap Install to confirm.
Instant WebAPK creation
C
Method 2: Via Chrome 3-Dot Menu (Fallback)
If the button didn't pop up immediately: Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome, then tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
D
All Done! Launch from Home Screen
The app icon is placed on your home screen and in your Android App Drawer. Once launched, the website navigation bar disappears into full-screen immersive mode!

Desktop Installation & Troubleshooting FAQ

Install on Windows PC, Mac, or Chromebook for an immersive, distraction-free desktop window.

1
Click the Install Icon in Address Bar
In Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave, look at the right edge of the address bar. Click the Install Icon (⊕ or desktop monitor).
2
Click "Install" to Confirm
A dialog will ask to install Project Bible in 92 Days. Click Install. The app launches immediately in a clean, standalone desktop window.
3
Pin to Windows Taskbar or Mac Dock
Right-click the Bible 92 icon on your taskbar/dock and choose "Pin to taskbar" (or "Keep in Dock") for one-click access every day!

Common Questions & Answers
❓ Why doesn't "Install App" show on iPhone?
Apple blocks websites from prompting automatically. You must use Safari's native Share ⎙ → Add to Home Screen button (see Page 1).
❓ Does the app work without internet?
Yes! The Service Worker caches the entire Bible portion, reader translations, and audio narration engine so you can read anywhere offline.
❓ Will it consume my phone storage?
No! Unlike 150MB App Store apps, this modern Progressive Web App uses under 2 MB total storage on your device.
❓ How do I get updates?
Updates install automatically in the background whenever you open the app while connected to the internet. No manual updates needed!
